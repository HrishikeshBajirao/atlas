## 🔭 Long-Term Vision

The goal is to build a country exploration platform where users can:

**Search**
→ Find countries quickly

**Compare**
→ Understand differences between countries

**Visualize**
→ Discover patterns in country data

**Explore**
→ Interact with countries geographically

**Analyze**
→ Use multiple datasets and visualizations to understand relationships and trends
